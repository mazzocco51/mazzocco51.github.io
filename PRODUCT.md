# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tech recruiters and hiring managers in AI/ML/quant, scanning fast (often mobile, often mid-pipeline). Job to be done: verify Marco is credible in under 60 seconds, then download the CV, open GitHub, or send an email. Secondary: hackathon/competition peers checking who they're talking to.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Marco Mazzocco — CS & Control Engineering student at Sapienza (Rome), focused on AI/ML, quant methods, and HPC. One page that converts a visit into a contact: CV download, GitHub click, or email. Success = a recruiter leaves with the CV and a clear picture of shipped work (WC2026 Edge Engine, Trading AI Multi-Agent) and competition record.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, confident. Brutalist-editorial: dark ground, hard 1.5px borders, monospace metadata labels, one hot red accent, outlined display type. Reads like an engineering datasheet with a pulse — precise, a little audacious, zero decoration for decoration's sake. Tagline energy: "Things that ship."

## Anti-references

- No slop: nothing that reads as template output — generic SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, soft rounded cards, cream/pastel "tasteful" neutrals.
- No corporate SaaS landing-page grammar (hero-metric blocks, identical feature-card grids).
- No Notion-style minimal portfolio (centered avatar, gray text, timid type).
- The existing numbered-section system (01–07) is deliberate brand voice, not scaffolding — keep it coherent, don't multiply it.

## Design Principles

1. **Datasheet, not brochure** — every element carries information (dates, scores, coordinates, stack tags); ornament is banned, density is welcome.
2. **One red, spent precisely** — `--red` marks action and achievement (links, CV tile, results); it never becomes wallpaper.
3. **Show shipped work** — projects lead with the question they answer and the stack that built them; claims are backed by artifacts (GitHub links, PDF, competition results).
4. **Convert fast** — CV download and contact links reachable without scrolling on any viewport; nothing delays the 60-second recruiter scan.
5. **Sharpen, don't soften** — refinements push the brutalist identity further (tighter grid, better rhythm, sharper type), never toward safe defaults.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Dark theme is the identity — maintain ≥4.5:1 body contrast against `#1c1c1b`. All motion (ticker, hovers) needs `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile. Semantic landmarks and alt text on all photos.
